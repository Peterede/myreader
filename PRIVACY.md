# MyReader privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-23_

MyReader is an offline ebook reader that speaks books aloud using the
text-to-speech engine already installed on your device.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** MyReader does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It
has no internet permission, contains no analytics or advertising SDKs, and does
not require an account.

## Data stored on your device

- **Imported books** — the EPUB or TXT files you choose are copied into the
  app's private storage so they can be read and narrated.
- **Reading positions and settings** — the current chapter/sentence, playback
  speed and chosen voice are saved locally so playback resumes where you left off.

This data never leaves your device. It is removed when you delete a book in the
library, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** — used to show a playback notification with play, pause and
  skip controls. You can deny this permission and still use the app.
- **Foreground service (media playback)** — keeps narration running when the app
  is in the background, with a visible notification.

## Third parties

MyReader calls the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. That engine
may be provided by Google or another vendor and is governed by its own privacy
policy; MyReader does not send it anything beyond the text being spoken.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including
children.
